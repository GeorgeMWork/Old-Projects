# Meat Market

Meat Market remains my most ambitious project, and that is precisely why it failed. My goal for this project was to create a shop-management style of game, but that very quickly spiralled out of control with feature-bloat. After a solid nine months of development, I had the beginnings of a game that was trying to be its very own Cyberpunk 2077.
Just without the experienced team, the funding, the support, etc. Naturally, it was very much out of my depth.  

Yet it was easily provided me with the most education and experience out of all my past projects. I realized I had been approaching a great many things incorrectly, and that, even when small in isolation, bugs and ramshackle design quickly added up to something quite monstrous to deal with.  

I considered spending the time to clean up what had been developed and very quickly decided against it. Not only was that a task in and of itself, it would have been a fruitless one. As previously stated, the game simply had far too much scope.  

Owing to the number of different features developed, I haven't recorded any footage of Meat Market. Instead, this will be the first project to have its code publicly available.

Features include the following:  
- NPC Simulation
- Organ Crafting
- Procedural Generation
- Object Pooling
- Inventory System
- Shop Management
- Currencies & Resources
- Injury Treatment
- Surgery System
- Character Statistics
- Combat & Movement
- Dialogue System
